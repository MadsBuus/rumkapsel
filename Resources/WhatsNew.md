## What's new in 0.58

- A teammate's workers stay through a short network drop, and a branch named for another repository's issue is no issue here
- Flat floor marks and a room's furniture no longer block the hallway beside it
- Walk the station at eye height, or ride along behind a minion on a crane
- A session archived in a checkout of its own takes its office with it
- An office with its lights off keeps its repository's colour in a rim, and borders stop at doorways
- Offices can open beside their own repository, and a station alone lays them out afresh
