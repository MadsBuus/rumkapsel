## What's new in 0.31

- The station apart from the picture. A `Simulation` with no SceneKit in it now owns the clock, the bodies and the orders they run, the walks, the rest and the idle life, the carries and office deliveries, the pallet errand, the shuttles' flights and the rockets' stages. The scene draws what it says, frame by frame, and decides nothing: a body's pose and load are facts on the body, a pallet, a flight and a rocket are numbers on the station clock, and what the scene shows once, a flush, a landing, a crate lifting onto the pallet, a rocket's flame, comes back as a cue. Nothing you see should have changed; what moves it has
- `--sim-tests`: the simulation stepped on its own, no window, through a send, a bath visit, a gym turn, a rest refused under a visit, the idle clock, the airlock, a carry from storage to the deck with the ledger in step, a wedged carrier giving up, and the whole pallet errand from the console to the deck
- The scenario gate runs in 12 seconds, down from 17: the station's fixed cells, a room placement's neighbour distances and the furniture's obstacle cells are worked out once rather than on every read
- A merged office's package is carried from where it actually stands, not from the office door
- Decon: a bot's pull request is an object of unknown origin in a chamber at the back of storage, come through a hatch that flashes, never by shuttle; merged, it is cleared and carried into storage by whoever is free, and from there it is a crate like any other; closed, it is ejected. The bots' room and its dependabot minion are gone. Decon stacks by repository, objects open their pull request on click, and low gravity in the yard: a crate with nothing under it takes two seconds to come down
- A closed issue never makes an office, whatever column the board left it in
- The pass is drawing only: the body walks its line exactly and never stops for anybody. From a tile off the walker slows and makes a quarter turn side-on, leans a shoulder to its own right for the length of the pass, and straightens and strides on once the other is clearly behind; two side-on figures clear each other within one tile. The sim gets ¼x and ½x for watching it, and Meet in the hall
- Fewer clocks: the carry's patience and the late set-down are gone, a crate is never moved by a clock, and a stalled carry is given up by the reconciler and requeued; the rocket's ninety-second moan with it
- Every sim press says now: Bath, Chore and Workout take a busy worker when nobody is resting, as Meet and Everyone to lounge already did
