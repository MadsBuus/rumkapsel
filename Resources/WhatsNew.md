## What's new in 0.55

- **A new flight.** A production deploy now flies a rocket built to be seen up close, with plated hull panels, the nose and a band in the repository's colour, its name along the side, and a blue flame held by six black flaps.
  - The liftoff is filmed from a camera on the rocket, then a long lens on the station follows it.
  - The empty first stage tumbles away at separation.
  - Side thrusters turn the rocket round for a braking burn, and the air glows against it on the way down.
  - The landing is filmed from the colony, where the rocket's flaps fold out as feet and it stands on the pad.
- **The flight window** opens at medium size. Click it to make it bigger; click the station to make it small again.
- **The gate between the deck and the pad is an X-ray** behind a red laser fence.
  - Crates cross only on the belt, and the operator checks each one on a monitor.
  - Anyone walking through stops at the fence to be scanned first.
- **The staging pallet** waits loaded on the deck until the staging deploy is live, then unloads. Its light turns red if the deploy fails.
- **A rocket is a tip and a lifter.**
  - The tip stands on staging on a cradle. A minion welds it together panel by panel while its staging deploy runs.
  - When a production release opens, the lifter rises out of the pad underneath it.
- **Work is shared out by importance.**
  - A message from you pulls your minion off lesser work. That work goes back on the list, and someone picks it up again later.
  - Packing a crate or stowing a commit waits until the office's minion is free.
- **Repositories that ship by tagging**, such as rumkapsel itself, keep merged work in storage. The rocket comes out when a new release is tagged.
- **Develop › Playbook** plays each of these moments on its own. It remembers where you put its window and opens behind your work.
