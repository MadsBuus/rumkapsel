## What's new in 0.62

- A rocket whose release is no longer cleared before it lights stands by again and calls off the carries it ordered aboard
- Minions' suits are a light grey, so the lights leave their sunlit faces white and shade the others, not one flat white
- Stepping into the walk fades in smoothly: no flat wall outlines at the start, the hull pieces fade out
- Walking, signs show the way: what lies down each arm of the plaza, where the main route forks, and the way back to the monolith from among the offices
- Minion suits are baked into the app; the repository's patch is worn over them, up close only
- The frame counter sits beside the window's buttons, not under them
- Up close, a crate shows its task's number, title and whose it is, in one of four cases; the rocket's plating is one size all over
- Walking, the hull comes down as glass onto a low steel sill
- Double-click the station's floor to walk in right there
- Minions read plain white from the station view; their suits show soft detail only up close
- Floors, crates and the station's rockets carry fine detail up close, and read as before from the station view
