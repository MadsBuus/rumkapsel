## What's new in 0.73

- Mission control by the pad: consoles whose chairs swivel to take a minion, a holo table, and a window onto the pad. Press M to walk in
- Its wall shows the station: a row of lamps per repository (deploy, checks, waiting, release, alarm) and live camera feeds of the pad and the bay
- A staging build counts down on its pad ring and nose lamp. When it's live the ring flashes, steam rings out, the nose turns green and a chime plays; a failed build smokes and turns red
- Pad rings keep their repository's colour instead of amber and red
- Minions bathe less, take turns in mission control, and a busy minion's bubble says it's busy
- The dorm, lounge, bath, gym and galley stay together where the plan puts them, and offices in the way move
- A deploy workflow is found wherever its branch is in the list, and the yard is counted again when its source changes
