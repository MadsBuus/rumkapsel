## What's new in 0.36

- A look may give minions tools of their own and draw a message its own way, and say whether working one throws sparks. The scene asks the look before it builds the hammer, the scanner, the torch, the tablet, the clipboard and the telekinesis wand, and binds the swing, the aim and the wand's tip and light by name, so a look's own tool moves the way the classic one does
- The HUD reads the look's background and inks itself dark on a light ground, so repo names, counts, the log and the status stay legible on grass; THEMES.md names every hook as it now is, the set pieces, worlds, tile context, launch and Shapes, and what a look still cannot do
- Shapes: flat organic shapes for a look. A field is sampled once over a rectangle and cut at any number of levels into flat meshes by marching squares, a row's run of full cells merged into one quad, so coastlines, clearings, yards and paths have soft edges instead of the grid's; a tile's patch runs on into its owner's neighbours and is cached by which of the eight they are; and Noise, keyed to world position, keeps a world where it was when the station round it grows. A run with no window draws no world at all, so a look's ground never slows the gate
- Looks follow the layers of a place. The protocol is ordered by them, the world, the input, the output, the center, the idle areas and the growth zone, and gains what the layers need: a look may draw the bay and the airlock, or decon, storage, the deck and the pad, whole as a set piece while the scene keeps the doors, the hatch, the console and every spot; it may hide a floor's square plane while the tile still names, dims and unfolds its detail; a tile's detail is told which of its eight neighbours share its owner; and a rocket leaves the way the look says. Under a theme of separate worlds each station is laid a world's gap from the next and the camera frames one at a time, switched with the station keys and the menu, cut to rather than flown to, and remembered. RK_DEMO_OFFICES adds offices to the demo to see a theme under load
- THEMES.md: stations are worlds, shown one at a time, the private station a world of its own rather than a site in a shared landscape or a quarter of one place; moving between worlds is still to design
- THEMES.md: how to design a theme. The six layers of a place (the input and the output as fixed set pieces, the center that says what the place is, idle areas as scenery, the growth zone, the world around it), stations as sites, designing for change, how theme work is judged against its references and across growth, where a theme lives in the code, what a look cannot do yet, and what the first medieval attempts taught. The README points to it
- A look can tell an office from the rooms every station has: a tile's floor kind gains fixed, for the lounge, the dorm, the bath and the gym, where they counted as offices
- A theme names the station's parts and doings in its own words: floor signs, hover text, orders and log lines read from a vocabulary on the theme, Classic's by default, and a theme changes only the words it draws as something else. Every test run pins Classic's words, since the scenarios expect log lines by what they say
- The fixed rooms' furniture comes from the look: the lounge, the bath, the gym and the beds, with the classic pieces as the default. A look hands back its pieces placed as the room stands, the solid ones and the fixtures apart, and the towel, the bar and the bag the scene moves; the scene names them as before, and a gym without a bar or a bag is simply not animated
- Every requirement of a look has a default, the classic station's, so a look says only what it changes: ClassicLook is empty, and the Kenney looks keep their overrides and fall back on the Classic pieces where a model is missing, where each kept a ClassicLook of its own to ask
- A look may draw a ship's path. A flight says how far through its phase it is, which slot it serves and which side it leaves toward, and the scene asks the look for a position and a heading before it falls back on the simulation's own path, which still says when a ship comes and onto which slot it lands
- The airlock runs five tiles through the hull and the bay hangs outside its far end, in every look, so the hatch is the station's edge and the bay no longer sits against it. The bay, its landing slots and the carriers' stands move out with the passage; a leaver cycles at the inner door, walks the passage and fades from the hatch on, where it faded as soon as the cycle ended. Layouts are saved as fleet-v22, since saved hallway and offices may stand where the passage now runs. --layout-tests holds, for every theme, that nothing else stands in the airlock or the bay and that the bay touches the station only at the hatch
- A look is told what kind of floor a tile is and what lies past each walled edge, and may drop the dark lines between owners. Kit, the Kenney model loader, moves to a file of its own, and the spots a look dresses — the pad's free corners, the parking beside the bay, an office's outer wall — are shared by any look that wants them. Decon floor has an owner like the rest of the yard, where every edge of it counted as open ground
- Themes live behind a Look: the scene asks the current look for every drawn piece and no longer branches on which theme is on. Theme is model-only (title, credit, and padGap, the one knob a theme turns on the plan); Looks/ holds the protocol, ClassicLook moved over as it was, and KenneyLook with its kit loader and ground, falling back to the classic piece when a model is missing. The layout tests pin Classic and run the yard checks for every theme; the settings credit names the Nature Kit too
- Shared stations converge. A peer's office is adopted where the peer put it: on free floor beside our hallway as before, and now also with the shortest dig through free floor to reach it, up to twelve tiles, where it used to be placed afresh and drift. When both machines placed the same room before hearing each other, the lower peer name's cells stand: every such room is cleared at once, since one often stands where another of theirs belongs, then theirs are adopted; hallway we dug under their floor is given back if what remains is one piece from the plaza with every door kept. The plan's own arms and alleys are never given back. --layout-tests holds two stations that placed one room differently to agreeing once the lower name's cells are taken, the hallway still whole and every room still with a door
