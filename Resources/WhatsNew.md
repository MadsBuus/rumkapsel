## What's new in 0.30

- The WC is a WC: a pedestal, a seat at sitting height with the hole marked, the tank on its back with a button, square to the walls where the four-sided bowl read as a diamond; the sitter sits square on it facing out from the tank, and its shadow goes onto the seat with it instead of staying on the spot. The shower's arm comes off a pole up the corner wall with a tap at waist height, and a towel hangs over a rail on the other wall
- The bubble's picks work by hand: the hover holds over a column from the minion's feet up to the plate, so the mouse can leave the body for the icons without the bubble going; a pick closes the bubble and lets the hover go, so the minion sets off at once; sent to bed from the bubble, a minion lies down once on its bunk whatever its session is up to, a nap flag on the body cleared by the next send elsewhere
- Walkers never stop for anybody: someone in the way is passed a third of a tile to the walker's own right, and within a sidestep of the waypoint the pass counts as done, since the bearing to a near waypoint swings and the spot beside it can never be chased to the point. The whole step is Walk.step, SceneKit-free; --walk-tests has two on one spot heading the same way, opposite ways, and a follower, all arriving. The idle soak passes three times running
- A toilet visit is a sit, not a stand: the minion turns its back to the bowl, its body bends into an L of torso, thighs and shins on the seat with the tank behind, shuffles on the seat now and then, straightens and stands up as the visit's last second runs out, and the bowl flickers water-blue behind it. The legs are boxes every figure carries unseen until it sits; the pixel patch rides along, lower, over the lap. The bowl's spot is one helper the fixture and the sitter both use. All on the order's own clock, no timers of its own
- A carrier that gives up sets the crate down behind it, on the way it came, never in the way ahead; the wedged-carrier scenario is back and checks what the rule promises, that the retry goes to somebody else, not that the crate lands past a body frozen in a storage row
- A lounger's next thing may be picked from its bubble: a row of five pixel glyphs under the words, a barbell, a bed, a book, a nozzle and a bowl, each in its own flat colour, drawn in code. A click gives the order through the calls the idle clock makes, takeTurnInGym, visitBath with showering set, or send to the lounge or the dorm, and hands the minion a fresh idle clock. The row is dimmed and clicks do nothing while a job is in hand, one waits, a crate is carried, or the minion is at work or mid-visit. The overlay gets first say on hovers and clicks, so the bubble stays while the mouse is over it; the hit rects are kept under a lock for the main thread. STATION.md: one line under the lounge rules
- Headless runs never take the keyboard: the model-only tests and a snapshot run as a background app like the scenario suite already did
- One rule for walkers meeting: drift a third of a tile to the side away from the other, pass, drift back onto the line, the other doing the same; hold still where there is no room, an arm's length off someone standing on the walk's last spot. Gone: right of way, the wait-then-replan, the third-of-a-tile yield, the idle bystander's step-aside, the doorway lanes and their claims, the replan throttle for meetings (the floor-change one stays). Walk.swift is SceneKit-free and --walk-tests runs it on made-up bodies; the wedged-carrier scenario is dropped, since it only passed by letting a walker slide onto an occupied spot. STATION.md's walking rules say the one rule
- The scenario suite is a smoke set again: ten scenarios that pinned incidents fixed days ago are gone (two source-lag cases the ledger tests cover, five walking and carrying incidents, three visit-length checks the soak still makes), fourteen flows remain and the gate runs in fifteen seconds. Visits last minutes, not seconds: a shower two to three, the toilet one, a gym turn four to eight, a look round two to four
- A visit carries its length on the order, so a bath or a gym turn that waited its turn in pending still lasts once there: the sub-second shower was begin() zeroing a length set after start(). Bathers and exercisers face their fixture, not the camera; the bench presser lies along the bench; nobody hops in the shower; the pixel pants are wider and coarser; the nozzle sits clear of a head with the drops under it. Body: the simulation's side of a minion as a SceneKit-free base class, Minion the figure on it
- A headless scenario run skips what only the eye reads: the view's tick (camera, labels, HUD) and the airlock pane's opening, which decide nothing and were a third of every tick; couch and bed spots are built once per floor plan, not once per resting minion per tick. The look-round scenario goes from 6.8 s to 1.7 s
- The idle pick and the doorway lanes as rules apart from the picture: IdleClock, IdlePick and RoamSpots in Idle.swift, DoorLanes and LaneClaims in Lanes.swift, no SceneKit in either; the scene calls them; --idle-tests and --lane-tests run them against a hand-stepped clock and made-up bodies
- Idle life that lasts: a visit's time starts on arrival; one idle clock per lounger picks a roam, a gym turn, the bath or the couch; doorways are one-lane sections with claims on the station clock; walkers who block each other resolve by right of way; re-planning throttled to once a second; scenario waits in station seconds with the idle soak off the default gate; model-only idle and lane tests; a fast local build mode; TESTING.md with the plan for model-only tests and the model-from-picture split
- Truth-state work in progress as found on the branch: doorway lanes with claims that lapse, one idle clock per lounger with a weighted pick, visits timed from arrival, gym and roam scenarios, and TESTING.md, the spec for what follows
