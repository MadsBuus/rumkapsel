## What's new in 0.69

- Subagents are probes: they launch from their minion, scan its office and fly to the monolith and back
- Every launch gets its own flight and screen, flown at once
- The camera stays where you left it, also after a restart
- Neighbours' repositories stay out of the titles at the top, and offices they left behind go after a day
- Crates on staging stay on the deck instead of being carried back to storage
- A crate's story says what happened, and leaves the waits to the clock
- A rocket waiting for a release links to what that release would carry
- After a restart no work moves until GitHub has answered, so stories get no phantom entries
- An office is no longer renamed to another repository's work and back
- Coming to the front asks GitHub what changed instead of reading everything again
