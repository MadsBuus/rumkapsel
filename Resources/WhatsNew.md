## What's new in 0.88

- A plain folder's office closes even when no session points at it
- A plain folder's session reports its branch as HEAD
- A session in a plain folder opens no office and gets no title
- Plain folders open in the repo list too, and never offer the Trash
- Repo actions sit in an Actions menu
- A repository's activity is your own sessions and offices only
- Titles that don't fit end in "+N more", which lists every repository
- A ship the production branch never saw is taken back
- Crates stand apart in their stacks, up to five high
- Every repository gets a title, newest first, and a click tells its story
- Releases into production stay in view: staging never becomes production
