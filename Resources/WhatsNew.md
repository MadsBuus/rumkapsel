## What's new in 0.44

- The bay's three landing pads sit across its middle rather than along its far edge, with the carriers waiting on the row by the hatch. A fixed room's name goes in the first corner its own furniture leaves clear, then the middle of a side, where it always went in the front corner: BAY was under a pad and LOUNGE under a couch. The lounge's plant and shelf stand against the back wall, clear of the side couches they stood inside.
- A rocket with everything already aboard says nothing about loading. It announced "loading 0 crates" on every launch before saying it was loaded; the load still runs, since that is what lowers the tape, but only the station log hears of it.
- A launch no longer erases the saved layout. The scene saved the fleet while setting itself up, a second before the first scan read the layout in, so an empty fleet was written over it every time: every office was placed afresh, teammates' offices came back by shuttle and were carried in again, and whatever the rocket had aboard was loaded a second time. The fleet now saves nothing until it has loaded.
- An office stays put while its workspace is open, rather than being archived and put back every few seconds. Two causes. The desktop app's worktree registry was checked through a URL, which keeps the first modification date it reads, so a lease taken after launch was never seen and the office counted as archived; the date is now read from the disk each time, and a registry caught half-written keeps the last whole answer. And sessions sharing one checkout each named the branch they last saw, so an old session put up an office for a branch long gone and the one-office rule took it down again on every scan; the session that wrote last now names the branch for all of them.
- A carrier left standing where the floor went away walks back onto it, and a crate put down off the floor lands on it instead. One stood in space with a crate on its arms for an hour, giving up and being sent again every twelve seconds: a walk could neither start nor end off the floor, and every give-up dropped the crate out of reach of anyone else. A walk from off the floor now goes straight to the nearest floor cell first, and a dropped crate lands on the nearest floor.
