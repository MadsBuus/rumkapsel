## What's new in 0.34

- The frame rate stays at 30 whenever the window can be seen, focused or not: a desk toy sits beside the work, and dropping to 8 the moment it lost focus made it lag in the corner of the eye. Only a covered or hidden window goes to 8
- The plan draws its dead-end alleys off the long arms again, every few runs, two or three tiles, on the side the arm last jogged away from, reserved like the arms and dug whole when a room wants the frontage; the passages between arms stay demand-dug. Judged on the forty and eighty room fills (RK_FILL=1 prints both, with mean walk, loops and footprint), that gives the crinkle back and a tighter footprint at the same mean walk. Charging the arms more past six steps and doubling the passage bonus changed nothing, so those knobs stay at their defaults. RK_FPS prints the frame rate the view is actually running at, which is how the low rate while hidden was checked: ten frames a second. A snapshot says how many nodes with geometry the scene holds
- A desk toy's diet: the audio engine is stopped outright while music is off, where it rendered silence forty times a second; the frame rate is 30 when watched, 60 only while a gesture drives the camera, and 8 while the window is covered or the app is in the background; antialiasing is 2X, which reads the same as 4X on flat shading and straight edges. The debug build went from 62% of a core to about 12%
