## What's new in 0.32

- build.sh looks for the release product in .build/out, where the native build system lays out a universal build; the old .build/apple path was left over from Xcode's own layout and made every release say the build failed
- The camera can go with one minion: a click follows it, keeping it in the middle while the zoom and the turn stay yours, with its order written along the bottom in its own words; Esc, a pan or a click on the floor lets go, and a double-click now does what a click did, asking GitHub again. A shower ends at the towel rail: the towel comes off the rail onto the shoulders for a few seconds, the water stops, and it goes back; the rail itself moved to the far end of the wall so it no longer reads as the nozzle from the camera's angle. The simulator's clock is two rows, pause and step as icons and the speeds under them, and the quarter and half buttons no longer light up with 1x. Headless runs ignore a cursor left over a minion, which is what froze a sitter in front of the bowl on film. For filming: --look frames a room, --follow keeps the camera on a minion, --frames and --every take a strip of pictures. AUDIT.md is re-checked against the simulation split, cited by function rather than line, with 18 rows moved upward and the open items kept; STATION.md's shower length matches the code. --sim-tests gains the bath's reservation, the sit on arrival and the drying beat
- build.sh removes last time's binary before compiling, so a failed compile says so instead of bundling the stale one
- The repo folder is ~/dev/rumkapsel now, and the demo station's entry for it says so too
- The long idle run is gone, and the rule it kept tripping: what it watched, that visits happen in a mix and last their whole time, the idle tests and the sim tests check in seconds, and two minions standing on one spot is no longer a rule of the station. The little disorder in storage and on an office's boxes is seeded from a hash that is the same in every run, where Swift's own hashValue was seeded afresh per process. The docs say where things stand: TESTING.md opens with the gate as one block to run, ARCHITECTURE.md's debts are the debts there are, and README.md says how to test and how to release, and that notarisation needs credentials stored once. The builds name the native build system: Xcode 26.1's default lays the products out where the debug binary cannot find Sparkle beside it
