## What's new in 0.54

- Production deploys fly: when a release merges, a camera window in the corner rides the repository's rocket up to its planet and lands at its colony when the deploy is live. The mission clock runs on how long the repository's GitHub Actions deploys usually take.
- A flight goes through liftoff, climbing, stage separation, course correction, coasting and the approach, and only shakes in the atmosphere.
- Click the window for a medium or a full view; full, drag or twist to turn the view a little. The × steps down to the small window, then closes it.
- Landed, click the colony's flag to open the release it flies.
- Deploys are read from GitHub every fifteen seconds without counting against its rate limit, and a merge is noticed within about ten.
- The window opens where you left it, on the screen you left it on.
