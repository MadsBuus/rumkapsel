## What's new in 0.57

- Idle orders show clear symbols and say what they do; a teammate is at work, not waiting for you
- Office names and owner stamps stay inside their own room
- Typing a share name no longer restarts sharing or rebuilds the station
- Stations reach each other on a shared network, and say why when they cannot
- The climb runs at the rocket's own pace, a rocket going up keeps its pad, and liftoff leaves soot
- With a board, only its column clears work, and a rocket standing by holds nothing
- A gate rebuilt with the floor takes its scan rings and belt crates with it
- Hallway no office needs any more is taken up again, from its far end inward
- A release that can't be notarised stops with Apple's reason instead of going out unnotarised
- The roadmap drops the release that went out and keeps the missing notarisation
- Idle is what the empty board leaves a body to, and nothing waits on a body any more
